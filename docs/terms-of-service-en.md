*Last updated: 2026-07-29*

## 1. Description of the service

HealthTrack is an app for self-monitoring health readings (blood pressure, pulse, SpO2): saving measurements, setting reminders, and viewing trends.

## 2. ⚠️ Medical disclaimer

**HealthTrack is not a medical diagnostic or treatment device.** All information in the app (including the "Insights" section) is for general informational purposes only — it is a simple calculation based on standard medical reference ranges, not a real AI or physician analysis. Always **consult a qualified doctor** before making decisions about your health. In an emergency, use the SOS feature and immediately call the appropriate emergency services (103, etc.).
