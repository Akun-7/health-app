# HealthTrack — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2026-07-29*

This document explains what data is collected, how it is stored, and how it is used when you use the HealthTrack mobile app (the "App"). By installing/using the App, you confirm that you have read this policy.

## 1. What data is collected

- **Account data:** your email address and a hash of your password (the password itself is never stored in plain text).
- **Health readings:** blood pressure, pulse, and SpO2 readings entered manually or via Bluetooth.
- **Profile data:** age, gender, weight, and other information you enter yourself.
- **Reminders:** medication/appointment reminders and their completion history (taken/skipped).
- **Chat messages:** if you message a user with the "doctor" role, the conversation is stored on the server.
- **Emergency contacts:** the name/phone number you enter for the SOS feature — this data is stored **only on your device** and is never sent to the server.
- **Language and settings:** interface language, theme (light/dark), and other preferences.

The App does not show ads and does not use analytics/tracking tools to monitor user behavior.

## 2. Where and how data is stored

- Health readings, profile, and reminders are stored on your device **encrypted with AES** — the key is kept in the OS's secure storage (Android Keystore/iOS Keychain).
- The same data is additionally copied to our cloud server (Render.com), so it can be recovered if your device is lost or damaged.
- ⚠️ **Important limitation:** the server currently runs on a free tier with no persistent disk — if the server restarts, the copy stored on the server may be lost (the copy on your device is unaffected). This is a temporary technical limitation that will be improved in the future.
- Password-reset codes are sent by email through a third-party provider, **Resend** (resend.com) — your email is shared with this service only for that purpose.

## 3. Third parties

The App uses the following third-party services:
- **Render.com** — server infrastructure (hosting)
- **Resend** — sends password-reset emails

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

## 4. Your rights

- You can delete your profile/measurement data from your device via Settings → "Clear all data".
- To fully delete your account (including data on the server), contact us using the details below.
- You have the right to request a copy of the data we hold about you.

## 5. Children

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 6. Security

Medical data is encrypted with AES, but no system can be 100% secure. Keep your device protected with a PIN or biometric lock.
